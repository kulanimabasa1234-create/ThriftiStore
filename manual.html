📖 User Manual

Getting Started

  • Create an account – Click "Log in" and then "Add new account".
  • Browse items – Use the category filters or search bar.
  • Buy an item – Click a product, then "Add to Cart" or "Buy Now".
  • Sell an item – Click "SELL" (must be logged in).
  • Wishlist – Save items by clicking the heart icon.

Managing Your Listings

  • Go to "My Listings" from the ⋮ menu to edit or delete your items.

Chatting with Others

  • On a product page, click "💬 Chat" to start a conversation.
  • All your chats are listed under "My Chats".

Admin Access

  • Log in with admin@thrifti.com (password you set) to see the Admin link.

Contact

  • For help, visit our Contact page.
